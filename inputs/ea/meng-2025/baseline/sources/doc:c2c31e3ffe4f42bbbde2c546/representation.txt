Key inclusion & exclusion criteria
Inclusion criteria: Patients =18 years of age undergoing elective classical open-heart surgery. Exclusion criteria: 1. Patients and their families refused to participate in the study; 2. Allergic to amide local anesthetics; 3. Patients who are unable to carry out normal communication and evaluation due to factors such as language impairment, hearing impairment, and mental illness; 4. Patients with grade IV heart function; 5. Pre-existing neurological disease; 6. Preoperative coagulation dysfunction; 7. Patients with infection in the puncture and catheter indwelling area; 8. People with sepsis. Age minimum: 18 Age maximum: 85 Gender: Both

Median open heart surgery
Intervention(s)
Control group:No PIFB will be performed. Intravenous analgesia pump (PCIA) will be used after surgery. The drug formula is Sufentanil 2.5µg/kg+ esketamine 1mg/kg+ dexmedetomidine 2.5ug/kg, diluted to 100ml with normal saline. Parameter setting: Background dose 2 ml/h, single dose 0.5ml, locking time 15 min.;Pectointercostal fascial block with a constant basal infusion:On the basis of the general anesthesia group, bilateral thoracic intercostal fascia block was added, 0.375% ropivacaine 20 ml was injected into each side, and an indwelling catheter 7-9cm was attached to an electronic analgesic pump with 0.12% ropivacaine 250ml. Parameter setting: Background dose 5ml /h.;Pectointercostal fascial block with a automated intermittent boluses infusion:On the basis of the general anesthesia group, bilateral thoracic intercostal fascia block was added, 0.375% ropivacaine 20 ml was injected into each side, an indwelling catheter was placed 7-9cm, an electronic analgesic pump was installed, and 0.12% ropivacaine 250ml was added into the pump. Parameter setting: background dose 0ml/h, pulse dose 20ml /240min.;
