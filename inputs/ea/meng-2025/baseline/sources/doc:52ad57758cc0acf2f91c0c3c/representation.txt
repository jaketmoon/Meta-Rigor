Study design: | Randomization: Randomized, Blinding: Triple blinded, Placebo: Used, Assignment: Parallel, Purpose: Prevention, Randomization description: These patients were randomized into two groups using permuted block randomization with 6 blocks (these patients are matched in terms of gender and age ±5 years). , Blinding description: In order to place the research units in two control and intervention groups and to create a random sequence, sealed envelopes were used. In this way, inside half of the envelopes, the letter c meaning the control group and inside the other half, the letter I meaning the intervention group. Then, each of the research units was asked to select an envelope to determine which group they belong to. The participant and the outcome evaluators and dsmb were unaware of the assigned group.

Intervention(s)
Intervention 1: Intervention group: In the intervention group, in addition to the common anesthesia protocol, loading dose: 6 mcg/kg/hr intravenous dexmedetomidine with maintenance drip of 0.2 mcg/kg/hour – 1 mcg/kg/hour will be injected. Intervention 2: Control group: common anesthesia protocol.
