Key inclusion & exclusion criteria
Inclusion criteria: 1.Patient giving consent 2.Age between 18-60years 3.ASA II & II 4.Patients undergoing elective cardiac surgery.(MVR,ASD) Exclusion criteria: Patients not giving consent. Patients with history of allergy to study drugs(ropivacaine,dexmedetomidine). Patients having abnormal coagulation profile Pre operative haemodynamic instability.(Congestive heart failure ,uncontrolled AF). Patients with moderate to severe PAH,LVEF less than 40%. Health Condition(s) or Problem(s) studied
Health Condition 1: I00-I99- Diseases of the circulatory system
Intervention(s)
Intervention1: Pectoralis block with Ropivacaine -Dexmedetomidine: 20ml of drug made with 0.125% ropivacaine and 1mcg/kg dexmedetomidine given before extubation on bilateral side in patients undergoing cardiac surgery.Block is given before shifting patient out od operation theatre and pain scores recorded for 24hrs post extubation. Control Intervention1: Pectoralis block with only Ropivacaine.: 20ml of 0.125% ropivacaine given bilaterally by pectoralis block before extubation in patients undergoing cardiac surgery.Block is given before shifting the patient out of operation theatre and pain scores recorded for 24hrs post extubation.
Primary Outcome(s)
1.Pain scores (VAS two hourly) in first 24 hours after extubation . 2.Total fentanyl(0.5mics/kg) consumption in first 24 hours. Timepoint: 1.Pain scores (VAS two hourly) in first 24 hours after extubation . 2.Total fentanyl(0.5mics/kg) consumption in first 24 hours.
Secondary Outcome(s)
1.Time of first rescue analgesia. 2.Length of icu stay. 3.Duration of mechanical ventilation after surgery. 4.Hemodynamic variables two hourly (MAP and heart rate). 5.Drug related side effects(delirium and sedation). Timepoint: Hemodynamic variables 2hourly (MAP and Heart rate)
