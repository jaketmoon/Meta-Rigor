Supplementary figure 1.

Predictive marginal probabilities for mRS 0-2 at 90 days

The predictive margins, with a 95% confidence interval, showed an intersection across all volume ranges of the two groups (EVT and MM) for achieving a favorable outcome (mRS 0-2 at 90 days). Confidence Intervals should be interpreted cautiously because of the small number in populations towards larger core volumes that may exceed 1·0.

EVT, endovascular therapy; MM, medical management; mRS, modified Rankin Scale.
