3.                                Supplemental eMethods

              A.                                                                 Inclusion Criteria

For inclusion in the study, participants fulfilled the following criteria:

                      •                                           Severe aortic stenosis (aortic valve peak velocity ≥4.0 m/s, or aortic valve area indexed to body
                             surface area <0.6cm2/m2 with aortic valve peak velocity ≥3.5m/s)
                      •                                           Age over 18 years
                      •                                           No symptoms attributable to aortic stenosis that require aortic valve replacement

              B.                                                                   Exclusion Criteria

Participants were not enrolled into the study if any of the following criteria were fulfilled:

                      •                                            Deemed lower risk for midwall fibrosis on screening
                      •                                           Planned cardiac surgery
                      •                                            Previous valve replacement
                      •                                            Severe hypertension (systolic >180 or diastolic >110 mmHg)
                      •                                           Acute pulmonary oedema or cardiogenic shock
                      •                                           Left ventricular ejection fraction <50% on cardiac MRI
                      •                                           Significant abnormalities on cardiac MRI that would prevent enrolment
                      •                                           Coexistent severe aortic regurgitation or mitral regurgitation
                      •                                           Coexistent mitral stenosis greater than mild in severity
                      •                                            Coexistent hypertrophic cardiomyopathy or cardiac amyloidosis
                      •                                           Any contraindication to MRI scanning (such as permanent pacemaker)
                      •                                            Advanced renal impairment (glomerular filtration rate <30 mL/min/1.73 m2)
                      •                                            Pregnancy or breast feeding
                      •                                     Patient judged to be unfit to be considered for aortic valve replacement or transcatheter aortic
                             valve implantation
                      •                                           Patient declines to consider undergoing valve replacement surgery or transcatheter aortic valve
                             implantation
                      •                                           Inability to give informed consent
                      •                                            Previous randomiz          ation into this study

              C.                                                                   Cardiac Magnetic Resonance Analysis
The presence of midwall late gadolinium enhancement as a marker of myocardial fibrosis was independently
assessed by the core laboratory blinded to clinical details. First, the core laboratory assessed each scan for
acceptable image quality according to standardized criteria. The presence or absence of midwall late gadolinium
enhancement was assessed qualitatively by two experienced readers (MD and RE, RB, KL or NC), and
differentiated from the pattern of scarring due to prior myocardial infarction, based upon short axis slices in
two-phase encoding directions and long-                     axis images (Supplement eTable 2). The opinion of a third independent
experienced reader was sought for any discordant classifications, and site investigators and attending physicians
were blinded to the cardiac magnetic resonance findings other than any unexpected clinically urgent findings  .

              D.                                                                   Details of co-enrolment with the EASY-AS Trial
During the recruitment phase of the trial, the EASY-AS trial opened to recruitment, randomizing patients with
asymptomatic severe aortic stenosis but not necessarily with myocardial fibrosis, to early aortic valve
intervention or guideline-directed conservative management (NCT04204915). The EVOLVED trial permitted
co-enrolment with the EASY-AS trial. Patients from EASY-AS who were eligible for EVOLVED took the trial
arm allocation from the EASY-AS randomization. The flow of patients through EVOLVED and EASY -AS is
shown on the following figure. In total, 47 patients were co -enrolled in the EVOLVED and EASY-AS trials.

           E.                                                                     Details of patients with midwall fibrosis withdrawn or excluded from analysis

One participant who was randomized to early aortic valve intervention was excluded from the modified
intention-to-treat analysis. This patient was randomized but subsequently identified to be ineligible because of a
referral for aortic valve surgery prior to being approached to participate in the trial.

A second participant randomized to guideline-directed conservative management was initially randomized in
error              on the test site rather than the live randomization site. Before they were randomized on the live site, their
eligibility was not re-confirmed, but they had developed symptoms and  had been referred by another
cardiologist for aortic valve intervention. When this was subsequently identified, the patient was withdrawn by
the Principal Investigator, without any follow-up being completed. As a result, the patient was also excluded
from the modified intention-to-treat analysis.

               B.          eFigure                        2: Trial Recruitment

           Recruitment to the trial was halted on 17 March 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic in line with a
           United Kingdom Government directive. Recruitment to the trial did not fully recover, and the results of
           the AVATAR and RECOVERY trials suggested a larger treatment effect of early intervention than had
           been assumed in the original power calculation. A decision to halt  recruitment into the trial on the pre-
           specified date of 31 October 2022 was therefore taken by the Trial Steering Committee.

© 2024 American Medical Association. All rights reserved.

                    B                                                             eFigure 3: New York Heart Association Symptom Classification

At one-year follow up, patients allocated to early intervention had a lower burden of
symptoms as assessed by the New York Heart Association classification compared to those
allocated to guideline-directed conservative management: adjusted odds ratio 0.37 [95%
confidence interval 0.20 to 0.70].
