Amendmen to statystical analysis
1. Patients randomized to AVR surgery who received delayed surgery
(more than 8 weeks after randomization) due to COVID -19 public
health emergency will be documented and this delay will be defined
as an anticipated minor deviation from the clinical protocol
2. Appropriate statistical procedures will be performed and results
will be published according to the FDA guidance "Conduct of Clinical
Trials of Medical Products During the COVID-19 Public Health
Emergency Guidance for Industry, investigators, and Institutional
Review Boards"
