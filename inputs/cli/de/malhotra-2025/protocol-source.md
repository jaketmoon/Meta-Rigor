# Reduced-dose versus standard-dose apixaban for extended treatment of cancer-associated venous thromboembolism: a systematic review and meta-analysis protocol

## Review objective

To compare the efficacy and safety of reduced-dose apixaban, 2.5 mg twice daily, with standard-dose apixaban, 5 mg twice daily, for extended anticoagulation and secondary prevention of venous thromboembolism in adults with active cancer.

## Review question

In adults with active cancer requiring extended anticoagulation for venous thromboembolism, how does apixaban 2.5 mg twice daily compare with apixaban 5 mg twice daily for recurrent venous thromboembolism, bleeding, and mortality?

## Eligibility criteria

### Population

Adults aged 18 years or older with active cancer and a history of venous thromboembolism, including deep-vein thrombosis or pulmonary embolism, who require extended anticoagulation after the initial six-month treatment phase. Studies limited to pediatric patients, patients without active cancer, or the first six months of anticoagulation will be excluded. Inpatient, outpatient, and ambulatory settings will be eligible without geographic restriction.

### Intervention

Reduced-dose apixaban, 2.5 mg twice daily.

### Comparator

Standard-dose apixaban, 5 mg twice daily.

### Study design

Randomized controlled trials directly comparing the two apixaban doses, reporting recurrent venous thromboembolism and bleeding outcomes, and following participants for at least six months. Studies of other anticoagulants, studies without adjudicated clinical outcomes, and indirect dose comparisons will be excluded.

## Information sources and search strategy

PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase via Ovid, CENTRAL, ClinicalTrials.gov, and Web of Science will be searched through 5 November 2025 without language restrictions. Controlled vocabulary and free-text terms will cover venous thromboembolism, cancer, anticoagulation, and apixaban. Reference lists of eligible reports and prior systematic reviews will be checked. Grey literature, including conference abstracts and dissertations, will not be searched.

## Study selection

Two reviewers will independently screen records and full texts in Rayyan. Disagreements will be resolved through discussion or consultation with a third reviewer.

## Outcomes

### Efficacy outcomes

- Recurrent venous thromboembolism, defined as objectively confirmed deep-vein thrombosis or pulmonary embolism after the initial six-month treatment phase.
- Deep-vein thrombosis.
- Pulmonary embolism.

### Safety outcomes

- Major bleeding defined according to International Society on Thrombosis and Haemostasis criteria.
- Clinically relevant non-major bleeding defined according to International Society on Thrombosis and Haemostasis criteria.
- All-cause mortality during extended follow-up.

### Composite outcomes

- Recurrent venous thromboembolism and major bleeding.
- Major bleeding and clinically relevant non-major bleeding.

## Data extraction

Two reviewers will independently extract trial identifiers, publication year, study design, sample size, participant demographics, cancer type, VTE subtype, follow-up duration, dosing strategy, baseline characteristics, and all eligible efficacy and safety outcomes using a standardized form. Disagreements will be resolved by a third reviewer.

## Risk-of-bias assessment

Risk of bias will be assessed using Cochrane RoB 2.

## Data synthesis

Random-effects meta-analysis will be performed in Review Manager 5.5. Risk ratios with 95% confidence intervals will be pooled using the Mantel-Haenszel method. Hazard ratios with 95% confidence intervals will be synthesized with a Knapp-Hartung adjustment. Between-study variance will be estimated using the DerSimonian-Laird method. Heterogeneity will be assessed using Cochran's Q, I², and τ².

## Reporting-bias assessment

Formal tests of funnel-plot asymmetry, including Egger's test, will not be performed when fewer than 10 studies are available because their results would be unreliable.

## Certainty of evidence

Certainty of evidence will be assessed using GRADE.
