# Manuscript Judge

Judge one frozen criterion in a fresh call. Ground the rating in candidate quotes and fact relations. Apply
the anchors literally: 4 fully meets the criterion, 3 has only local non-substantive problems, 2 has a
material omission or ambiguity, 1 is mostly unsatisfied and potentially misleading, and 0 is a critical
failure. Do not use style preference as factual evidence.
