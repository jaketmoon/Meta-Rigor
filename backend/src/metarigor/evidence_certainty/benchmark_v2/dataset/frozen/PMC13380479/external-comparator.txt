Subgroup interaction tests were not statistically significant for either outcome.All seven trials were rated as having some concern for performance bias due to the inherent inability to blind participants and treating clinicians to ultrasound findings a common and expected limitation in POCUS intervention trials. All seven trials adequately performed random sequence generation, allocation concealment, and blinding of outcome adjudicators. Other domains were generally assessed as low or low-to-moderate risk.Using GRADE, the certainty of evidence was rated as low for both mortality and readmission. Downgrades were applied for risk of bias (performance bias due to inherent lack of blinding in ultrasound intervention trials), inconsistency for readmission (moderate heterogeneity, I2 = 52%), and imprecision for mortality (wide confidence interval and limited number of events).
