CONCLUSIONS
Extended anticoagulation with reduced-dose apixaban was noninferior to full-dose
apixaban for the prevention of recurrent venous thromboembolism in patients with
active cancer. The reduced dose led to a lower incidence of clinically relevant bleed-
ing complications than the full dose. (Funded by the Bristol-Myers Squibb–Pfizer
Alliance; API-CAT ClinicalTrials.gov number, NCT03692065.)
