MATERIALS AND METHODS
This study was conducted under an Icahn School of Medicine at Mount
Sinai Institutional Review Board–approved protocol with informed consent.
Patients
Adult patients (aged >18 years) undergoing elective cardiac surgery
requiring CPB were studied. The exclusion criteria were (1) severe
preoperative cognitive impairment by history or preoperative cognitive
testing; (2) sensory or motor impairment precluding reliable operation of
a computer; (3) lack of access to computer-based cognitive evaluation;
(4) non–English-speaking patients; (5) renal failure requiring dialysis;
(6) respiratory failure requiring home oxygen use; (7) Child’s B or C
hepatic failure; (8) procedures with deep hypothermic circulatory arrest;
and (9) baseline oximetry values less than 60%. Eligible patients who
consented to participate and met the enrollment criterion of completing
preoperative cognitive testing were assigned to an intervention or control
group differing with respect to rSO treatment algorithms (described VIDEO 1. Cerebral oximetry demonstration and study explanation on a
